Our story

Same essence. A new experience

Caring for people's health for more than 40 years, Extremamedic has evolved into MedicGLOBAL. Our legal name and our registration with the ANS (Brazil's National Supplementary Health Agency) remain the same — what has changed is the way we care.

Timeline

More than 40 years of history,
caring for people's health

1981

Hospital São Lucas is founded

Our history of caring for people's health in the region begins.

1984

Extremamedic is founded

Offering outpatient health plans to the market.

2015

12,000 members

Extremamedic reaches the milestone of 12,000 members under its management.

2017

Partnership with SERPRAM

Extremamedic partners with SERPRAM and begins offering hospital plans to the market.

2018

Hospital São Lucas expanded

Expansion and modernization of Hospital São Lucas.

2021

Sale of SERPRAM

SERPRAM is sold to GNDI.

2021

New departments at Hospital São Lucas

Diagnostic Imaging, Hemodynamics and Ophthalmology departments are opened.

2022

End of the partnership

The partnership between Extremamedic and SERPRAM comes to an end.

2024

Back to our roots

Extremamedic launches new hospital plans on the market.

2024

MedicGLOBAL is born

A new name, with plans whose coverage area extends to Pouso Alegre/MG — keeping the same legal name and the same ANS registration (No. {{L.ans}}).

Where we operate

From southern Minas Gerais to the Bragança Paulista region

Headquartered in Extrema/MG, MedicGLOBAL operates between Bragança Paulista/SP and Pouso Alegre/MG — and offers urgent and emergency care while traveling anywhere in Brazil through the ABRAMGE System.
