Circuito de Cuidado da Mulher (Women's Care Circuit) offers exams and care nationwide

Informational reference · Source: Ministry of Health (Brazil) (opens in a new tab)

On October 17, the Brazilian Ministry of Health, in partnership with states and municipalities, held the first edition of the Circuito de Cuidado da Mulher (Women's Care Circuit), with activities in several locations across the country.

What was offered

The initiative brought together care, exams, contraceptive methods, vaccines and discussion groups. Services included the HPV DNA test, mammography when indicated, and IUD and subdermal implant insertion.

Some locations received the Carretas de Saúde da Mulher (Women's Health mobile units), offering mammography, ultrasound and colposcopy, with the goal of reducing waiting lists in the municipalities.

Dignidade Menstrual (Menstrual Dignity)

The Dignidade Menstrual (Menstrual Dignity) Program was also part of the initiative: eligible women could receive authorization to obtain free sanitary pads.

Guidance for professionals

To support health managers, Joint Technical Note No. 360/2026 was published. The Papo de Mulher series, with three thematic booklets, was made available to health professionals.
