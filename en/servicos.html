Urgent and emergency care

In an urgent situation, know what to do

192SAMU (emergency ambulance), if life is at immediate risk
  • Immediate risk to life? Call 192 (SAMU) or go to the nearest emergency room.
  • Within the coverage area: check the hospitals in the provider network.
  • While traveling: urgent and emergency care while traveling through the ABRAMGE System.
  • Urgent authorization: authorizations WhatsApp (35) 9 8845-9074.

Accessibility

Sign language and reading

Automatic ASL translation is not available on this site yet. The VLibras tool used on the Portuguese version translates Portuguese into Libras (Brazilian Sign Language) only, so it is not offered here.

If you are Deaf or hard of hearing, you can reach us in writing on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab) or through the other customer service channels. Libras users can switch to Português to use VLibras.
