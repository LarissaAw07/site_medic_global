Ombudsman

Your voice is heard

A member service channel available on business days, Monday through Friday, from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

The Ombudsman is the second level of service: contact it when your request has not been resolved through our customer service channels. A conclusive answer is sent within 7 business days; in more complex cases, the deadline may be agreed with you and extended to up to 30 business days.

  • (35) 3435-4442
  • ouvidoria@extremamedic.com.br
  • Provide your protocol number, if you already have one.

If your request is not resolved by the Ombudsman, you can escalate it to ANS (Brazil's National Supplementary Health Agency): Disque ANS 0800 701 9656 or gov.br/ans (opens in a new tab).

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Answers to the questions members ask most, in just a few clicks.

Didn't find what you were looking for?

Contact customer service
How do I get a duplicate payment slip?
Go to the payment slip system. If you have questions, contact billing on WhatsApp at (35) 9 9201-0023.
How do I request an authorization?
Send your request through the authorizations WhatsApp line (35) 9 8845-9074, including the health professional's order and the member's details.
How do I find a doctor or laboratory?
Use the provider network search or the MedicGLOBAL app.
How do I access telemedicine?
See the step-by-step guide on the telemedicine page.
I forgot my Member Area password.
Use the Forgot my password option. If this is your first access, complete the initial registration.
When should I contact the Ombudsman?
When your request has not been resolved through our customer service channels. The Ombudsman is available Business days, Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m..

Accessibility

Sign language and reading

Automatic ASL translation is not available on this site yet. The VLibras tool used on the Portuguese version translates Portuguese into Libras (Brazilian Sign Language) only, so it is not offered here.

If you are Deaf or hard of hearing, you can reach us in writing on WhatsApp (opens in a new tab) or through the other customer service channels. Libras users can switch to Português to use VLibras.
