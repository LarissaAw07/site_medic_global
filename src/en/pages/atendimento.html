Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

How do I get a duplicate payment slip?
Go to the payment slip system. If you have questions, contact billing on WhatsApp at {{L.wa.financeiro.label}}.
How do I request an authorization?
Send your request through the authorizations WhatsApp line {{L.wa.autorizacoes.label}}, including the health professional's order and the member's details.
How do I find a doctor or laboratory?
Use the provider network search or the MedicGLOBAL app.
How do I access telemedicine?
See the step-by-step guide on the telemedicine page.
I forgot my Member Area password.
Use the Forgot my password option. If this is your first access, complete the initial registration.
When should I contact the Ombudsman?
When your request has not been resolved through our customer service channels. The Ombudsman is available {{L.phones.ouvidoriaHours}}.
