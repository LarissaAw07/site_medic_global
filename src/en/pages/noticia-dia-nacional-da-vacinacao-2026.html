National Vaccination Day reinforces protection against more than 20 diseases

Informational reference · Source: Ministry of Health (Brazil) (opens in a new tab)

Ahead of National Vaccination Day, observed on October 17, the Brazilian Ministry of Health highlighted its support for states and municipalities to expand protection for the population.

Doses and coverage

In 2026, the Ministry of Health has already distributed more than 222 million doses. The SUS (Brazil's public health system) offers free vaccines against more than 20 diseases.

In 2025, coverage of the first dose of the MMR vaccine reached 93.10%. WHO and UNICEF data released in July show that the number of children without the first dose of the pentavalent vaccine fell from 360,000 to 50,000 between 2023 and 2025, a reduction of about 90%.

Measles

In 2024, Brazil regained its measles-free certification, which it had lost in 2019, a year with more than 20,000 cases. In 2026, 61 imported or import-related cases were recorded.

When to vaccinate

The MMR vaccine is given at 12 months (first dose) and at 15 months (second dose). Not having a vaccination card does not prevent vaccines from being given.
