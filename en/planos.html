FAQ

Before you sign up

What is the difference between the BLUE 200, 300 and 400 plans?
The main stated difference is the geographic coverage: BLUE 200 is regional, from Bragança Paulista/SP to Cambuí/MG; BLUE 300 and BLUE 400 cover from Bragança Paulista/SP to Pouso Alegre/MG. The other features of each product are presented by our sales team.
Do the plans include hospital coverage with obstetrics?
MedicGLOBAL offers products with outpatient care and hospital care with obstetrics, in accordance with the List of Procedures (Rol) of the ANS (Brazil's National Supplementary Health Agency). The coverage segment of each product is confirmed in the quote and in the contract.
What if I need care outside my region?
Urgent and emergency care while traveling is available through the ABRAMGE System, according to the terms of your contract. Learn how it works.
How do waiting periods work?
Waiting periods follow ANS regulations and are set out in each product's contract. Check the plan's terms and features with our team.
How do I sign up?
Fill out the form below or contact our sales team on WhatsApp at (35) 3435-4442. Our team will get in touch to understand your profile and present the available options.
Where can I check the rate adjustments applied?
The rate adjustment percentages for the contract pool (RN 565) are published in the document center.
