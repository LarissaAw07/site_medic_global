Medical health plans surpass 53 million members in August

Informational reference · Source: ANS (opens in a new tab)

ANS (Brazil's National Supplementary Health Agency) released its health plan membership report for August 2026, with the total number of enrollments and the movement of new enrollments and cancellations over the past 12 months.

August figures

In August 2026, medical health plans recorded 53,164,516 members. Dental-only plans totaled 37,311,529.

In that month alone, medical plans had 1,237,646 new enrollments and 1,175,486 cancellations, which corresponds to a turnover rate of 2.21%.

12-month movement

Over the past 12 months, medical plans had 16,117,456 new enrollments and 15,434,267 cancellations, with a turnover rate of 29.41%.

How to read these data

The figures refer to contractual enrollments, not to individuals, since the same member may have more than one plan. ANS also notes that the data may be revised retroactively, due to the monthly revisions submitted by health plan operators.

Original source
