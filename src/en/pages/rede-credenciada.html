Before you book

Check your plan’s coverage area and coverage

The services of the professionals and providers listed in the network are available to regulated plans (those issued after Law No. 9,656/98), subject to the coverage area and contractual coverage of each plan type.

If you are a member, check your contract’s coverage area in the Member Area or contact customer service.
