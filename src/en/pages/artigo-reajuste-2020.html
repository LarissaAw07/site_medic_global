2020 health plan rate adjustment recovery

{{pic:old_comunicado|MedicGLOBAL notice artwork with the text “Urgent notice”|2000|(min-width:1360px) 1300px, 100vw|eager}}

In August 2020, the ANS (Brazil's National Supplementary Health Agency) decided to suspend the application of health plan rate adjustments from September to December 2020.

What was decided

The decision was made after the ANS Collegiate Board's 16th Extraordinary Meeting, as a result of the pandemic caused by the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus. The suspension covered both cost-variation (annual) adjustments and age-bracket change adjustments.

Where the guidance was published

The guidance for health plan operators to comply with the decision was published in the Official Gazette (Diário Oficial da União) on September 2, 2020, through ANS Notice No. 85.

Still have questions?

Contact customer service or check the rate adjustment percentages in the document center.

Source: ANS — Notice No. 85, Official Gazette (DOU) of Sep 2, 2020.
